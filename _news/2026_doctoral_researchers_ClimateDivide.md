I am delighted to welcome [Miriam Runde](https://climatedivide.eu/author/miriam-runde/) and [Angelika Juhász](https://climatedivide.eu/author/angelika-juhasz/) as doctoral researchers in my Emmy Noether project **The New Climate Divide** at Heidelberg University. Miriam examines how climate change issues shape citizens' political participation in Europe, while Angelika investigates how opposing political group identities form around climate change. Read more on the [project website](https://climatedivide.eu/news/2026-10-01-doctoral-researchers-join/).
