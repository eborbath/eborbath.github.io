Next week I will attend the [32nd International Conference of Europeanists](https://www.ucd.ie/dei/newsandevents/events/ucdhoststhe32ndinternationalconferenceofeuropeanists16-18june2026/){:target="_blank"} in Dublin (June 16-18, 2026) and the [European Political Science Society conference](https://epssnet.org/belfast-2026/){:target="_blank"} in Belfast (June 18-20, 2026).
<br>
At CES, Dino Noah Wildi and I will present two papers: <i>"From Valence to Cleavage: Conceptualizing the Climate Divide as Political Mobilisation Issue"</i> and <i>"Automated Political Claims Analysis With LLMs"</i>. In Belfast, these two papers will be joined by a third paper, <i>"Movement Parties and Parliamentary Speech in Chile and Spain"</i>, co-authored with Daniel Saldivia Gonzatti and Jan Schwalbach.
